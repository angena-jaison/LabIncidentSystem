Chemical Spill Response Guidelines

For small spills (under 1 liter) of non-hazardous or mildly irritant chemicals, lab staff
may clean the spill themselves using the spill kit located under each fume hood. Always
wear gloves and safety goggles before approaching a spill.

For any spill involving a corrosive, flammable, or unknown substance, evacuate the
immediate area, alert nearby staff, and contact the safety officer before attempting any
cleanup. Do not use the standard spill kit on chemicals marked hazardous without
supervisor approval.

Every chemical spill, regardless of size, must be logged as an incident, including the
substance involved, approximate volume, and how it was cleaned up. This record helps
identify equipment or containers that repeatedly fail and should be replaced.
