Centrifuge Safety Procedure

All centrifuges must be balanced before every run. Place sample tubes directly opposite
each other on the rotor, matched by weight within 0.1 grams. An unbalanced rotor is the
most common cause of vibration, bearing damage, and overheating during a run.

If a centrifuge begins to overheat, emit an unusual smell, or make a grinding noise,
immediately press the emergency stop button and do not open the lid until the rotor has
come to a complete stop. Do not attempt to force the lid open while the rotor is spinning.

After an overheating event, tag the equipment as "Out of Service" and notify the lab
manager. The unit must not be used again until it has been inspected by a qualified
technician, the motor and bearing temperatures have been checked, and the incident has
been logged in the incident management system.

Routine maintenance should be performed every three months, including checking the rotor
for cracks, lubricating the lid seal, and verifying the emergency stop mechanism.
